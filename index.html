How to interpret this screening result

This app applies simple Ho/He and Fis thresholds. Ho greater than He alone is not a formal bottleneck test; proper bottleneck methods compare heterozygosity with an expectation conditioned on allele count under a mutation-drift model. Population structure, relatedness, sample design, and genotyping quality can also change these summaries.
